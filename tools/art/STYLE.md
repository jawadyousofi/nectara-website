# Nectara supporting art: style guide

Used for every AI-made supporting image (children, scenes, story pictures). Asal and other signature characters stay
human-made or human-reworked so they can be copyrighted; this guide is for the supporting cast and scenes.

**Look:** soft 3D cartoon, like a gentle animated film for preschoolers. Rounded, chunky shapes; big expressive eyes with
one bright sparkle; rosy cheeks; small nose; warm smile. Soft, even daylight, no harsh shadows. Matte, slightly velvety
surfaces. Colors warm and natural: honey yellow, leaf green, sky blue, soft coral, warm earth browns.

**Children:** ages 4 to 6, proportions like Asal (big head, small rounded body), friendly and kind, never scary.
Every child is distinct and real: skin tones, hair types and features from all over the world, never stereotyped.
Simple, comfortable clothes in Nectara colors.

**Never:** text or letters in the image, logos or brand names, weapons, anything frightening, adult themes,
photorealistic human faces, gore or medical realism.

**Format:** transparent background for characters (PNG); full scenes at 16:9.
