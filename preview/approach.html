How we learn

Wonder first. Always.

Children are not empty jars to be filled with other people’s answers. They are seeds that already hold everything they need. Our work is to give them sun, soil, water and time.

Six things we believe

Each child has an inner power

We help children notice what lights them up, and give it room to grow at its own pace.

The garden is a classroom

Herbs, roots, mushrooms and trees teach patience, care and how a living system heals itself.

The body is a wonder

Every child began as one droplet of life. We learn how our bodies move, rest, breathe and grow, and why.

Feelings have names

Children learn to name what they feel, be with it, and find their way back to calm.

The whole world is home

Food, music, stories and games from many cultures, so every child feels like a citizen of the earth.

Parents grow too

Families learn alongside their children. A hive is stronger when everyone in it is growing.

A day at Nectara

The rhythm of a day

Like a bee’s day, ours moves outward to explore and returns home to share. Garden time and brain-stimulating studio time take turns, so bodies and minds both stay awake.

  • ArriveQuiet breathing, a short poem, and each child names how they feel.
  • GardenPlanting, watering, digging for roots and asking one big question together.
  • MoveRunning, balancing, climbing and dancing: learning the body by using it.
  • MakeBuilding, puzzles, drawing, and early letters and numbers through stories.
  • WorldA story, song or dish from another culture.
  • CircleWhat did I notice? What am I grateful for? What do I still wonder about?

What you won’t find here

Worksheets for toddlers. Rankings. Rushing. Our children are not in a race, and neither are their families.

What you will

Muddy knees, quiet minutes, a lot of questions, and children who know how to calm themselves, care for a plant and make a friend.
