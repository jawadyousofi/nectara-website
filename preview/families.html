For families

You are your child’s first teacher.

We don’t take that role from you. We share it. Nectara families get simple ways to bring the garden, the breath and the wonder home.

Take-home seeds

Each week, one small idea to try at home: a plant to water, a breath to practice, a question to ask at dinner.

Family circles

Regular gatherings where parents share, learn and slow down together, with the children close by.

An open door

Clear, warm communication about how your child is growing, in their body, their feelings and their curiosity.

Questions families ask

What ages does Nectara serve?

Children ages 2 to 7, in three bands: Seeds (2–3), Sprouts (4–5) and Blossoms (6–7).

When and where will Nectara open?

We’re starting with small sessions in local parks and community spaces, plus activities families can do at home. Our own garden and classroom come next. Founding families hear every date and location first.

What does it cost?

Tuition is being set now and will be shared with founding families first, along with any founding-family options.

Is Nectara licensed?

We’re working through the licensing and approvals that apply to our first program and location. We’ll share exactly what is in place before any program begins.

Do children use screens?

Rarely, and only with purpose: a short guided video or a call with friends from another part of the world. Most of the day happens in the garden and the studio.

What should my child wear?

Clothes that can get muddy and shoes for the garden. We go outside in every season.

Can parents take part?

Yes. Parents of our youngest children are often close by, and every family is invited to family circles and garden mornings.
