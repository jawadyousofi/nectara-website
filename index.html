A learning community for ages 2–7

Where every child blooms.

Like bees gathering nectar from many flowers, our children gather wonder from everything around them: the soil and the stars, their own breath and bodies, and the cultures of every corner of the earth.

Learning the way nature lives

Days move between the garden and a warm indoor studio, in every season, rain or shine. No one races to beat anyone. Like the hive, we grow together.

The garden

Seeds, roots, herbs, mushrooms and bees: how life grows, heals and begins again.

Breath and body

Calm breathing, movement and feelings: understanding the body that grew from one tiny drop of life.

One world

Stories, songs, food and games from every culture, so children feel at home anywhere on earth.

A day at Nectara

A gentle rhythm that leaves room for wonder

Each day is a loop from stillness to discovery and back again, with the garden at its heart.

See the whole day
  • ArriveQuiet breathing, a short poem, and each child names how they feel.
  • GardenPlanting, watering, digging for roots and asking one big question together.
  • CircleWhat did I notice? What am I grateful for? What do I still wonder about?
