Meet Asal

Hi! I’m Asal, the little bee.

Asal means honey. I love flowers, rain, big questions and my friends. I fly from flower to flower to gather nectar, and I fly from question to question to gather wonder.

Say it with Asal

Asal’s breathing song

Say it slowly. Breathe in on the first line, out on the next.

Buzz in, breathe in,buzz out, breathe out.I am small,I am strong,I help the garden grow.
